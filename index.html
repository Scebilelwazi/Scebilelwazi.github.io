About Me

Hi, I'm Nolwazi Mahlambi – a final-year BSc IT student at North-West University with a deep passion for cybersecurity. I'm fascinated by how systems work and how to protect them from threats.

My interest in cybersecurity started when I began exploring TryHackMe labs and Cisco networking courses. I realized that I love the challenge of finding vulnerabilities, thinking like an attacker, and building defenses to protect systems.

I'm currently building my skills in network security, ethical hacking, vulnerability assessment, and incident response. My goal is to become a Junior SOC Analyst who helps organizations detect, respond to, and recover from cyber threats.

Outside of studies and certifications, I enjoy participating in CTF challenges, learning new tools, and staying updated on the latest security breaches and trends. I believe that cybersecurity is not just a career – it's a mindset.
